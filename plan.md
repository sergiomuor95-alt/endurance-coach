## How to read this

- Running (km) is total weekly running volume. Cycling and strength are tracked separately, they flex by phase (see table below).
- Long run is Saturday or Sunday, whichever your week suits.
- Tue Apex, Wed RCC, Fri Apex stay in place all 20 weeks. What changes by phase is the effort level, not whether they happen.
- Cutback weeks are not optional. If a peak week isn't going well, shorten that week's long run before you touch a future cutback week.

## Reality check on sub-3:10

Riegel formula off your 1:34:00 half predicts roughly a 3:16 marathon at current fitness. Sub-3:10 is realistic, but it's not sitting there on fitness alone. It needs the volume below and the pacing discipline Tokyo didn't have. Going out at 3:10 pace from the gun is what put you on the ground at 32km last time.

## The volume jump

Steady state is ~32km/week, last 4 weeks 35.6km/week. Peaking at 100km/week is roughly 3x that. It's a normal peak for a sub-3:10 target, the issue is the rate of getting there, not the number. This plan ramps over 14 weeks with 3 cutback weeks built in specifically for that. Don't compress it even if early weeks feel easy.

## Cycling and strength by phase

| Phase | Wed RCC | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation & Build (Wk 1-12) | Ride as normal | 2x/week, hypertrophy focus, plus ankle stability work each session |
| Peak (Wk 13-17) | Keep it, but ride within yourself, it's not a training stimulus day | 1x/week, maintenance only, ankle/glute/calf stability is the priority |
| Taper (Wk 18-20) | Easy spin or skip, your call | Mobility and activation only, no loading |

Muscle-building goal pauses during peak weeks, recovery capacity goes to running. It resumes as the priority after London. Ankle stability work stays non-negotiable throughout given the May 2025 sprain, it's what keeps you upright at 90km+ weeks.

## Pacing and fueling, the Tokyo fix

- Sub-3:10 works out to about 4:30/km average.
- Don't leave the start line at goal pace. First half slightly slower than average, second half at or under it.
- Start rehearsing race-day carb intake in the Phase 3 long runs (week 9 on), not for the first time on race day. 60-90g carbs/hour is the standard endurance benchmark to test and adjust from.
- Sydney base training runs hot, London won't. Don't let heat-adapted pacing talk you into starting too conservatively either, worth knowing your cool-weather paces separately if you get the chance to test them.
